# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct
that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to unacceptable behaviour. Reports can be sent
privately via a GitHub security advisory or a direct message to the maintainers.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
